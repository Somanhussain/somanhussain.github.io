🎯 What Is Easy-Worship?

Easy-Worship is a desktop configuration utility designed for churches that project song lyrics, scripture references, and announcements during a live service. Instead of clicking through dozens of scattered settings every Sunday, you get one clean place to set things up once and reuse it week after week.

The application focuses on the small details that make a big difference in a live environment: how your lyric library is organised, how quickly a volunteer can find the next song, and how consistently your presentation looks on screen. Whether you are running a single projector in a small chapel or a multi-screen setup in a larger sanctuary, the utility gives you a simple, dependable starting point.

You do not need any technical background to use it. If you can install a normal Windows program and click a few buttons, you can configure Easy-Worship for your team.

✨ Key Features

  • Guided configuration: A step-by-step setup area walks you through the most important options so nothing important gets missed before a service.
  • Worship lyrics organisation: Keep your praise and worship song library tidy, grouped, and easy to browse when the band changes the set list at the last minute.
  • Church presentation presets: Save your preferred layout and text settings as a preset and reuse it instantly for every service.
  • Simple desktop interface: Large buttons, clear labels, and readable text designed for volunteers who are using the computer for the first time.
  • Fast setup utility: Prepare a brand-new laptop or a replacement machine quickly instead of rebuilding every setting by hand.

  • Reusable profiles: Set things up once for your main sanctuary and again for a youth hall or overflow room, then switch between them in seconds.

🛠️ First-Time Setup Tips

Your first ten minutes with the utility set the tone for every service after that. Here is a simple routine that works well for most churches.

  • Start with one profile. Create a single configuration for your main room rather than trying to cover every venue at once. You can duplicate and adjust it later.
  • Add your most-used songs first. Enter the five or six praise and worship songs your team plays almost every week. That is enough to test the flow end to end.
  • Check your text size on the real screen. Open a lyric on the actual projector or TV, not just your laptop monitor. Sit in the back row if you can — if you can read it from there, the congregation can too.
  • Save a backup copy. Once your library and settings look right, copy the configuration folder to a USB drive or church cloud storage. If the presentation laptop ever fails, you are back online in minutes.
  • Write down your choices. Keep a short note of the settings you picked so a new volunteer can understand the setup without hunting through menus.
